## Critical lifecycle and audience decisions
The legacy student audience ALL/PLACEMENTS/batches/branches/drive is centered on learners; the vNext general COLLEGE send uses all active college memberships, potentially including nonlearners. Review business rules rather than treating recipient count equality as proven. Legacy From42 auto-acknowledgment is per faculty user on GET; vNext explicit POST records first college reader (one receipt per college), changing unread semantics. The newer 30-second server delivery hold, rate limit, CAS and idempotency are meaningful safety additions, but scheduled undo origin (`createdAt` vs `sentAt`), recipient snapshot, and remind-owner restriction require explicit product signoff.
