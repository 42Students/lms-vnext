### 1.3 Server row model (HUB:81-141)
Per lead: id, name, designation, companyName/logo, email, phone, manualStage, notNowReason/Until, owner id and name, source, recruiterId, inviteSentAt/ClickedAt/BouncedAt, unsubscribedAt, createdAt, last activity (type, note, time; any activity incl. system), next follow-up (earliest open), verification, recruiter last login, and derived `drives`, `studentsHired`, `stage`, `inviteStatus`, `chips`, `quietDays`. Order createdAt DESC.
- Company numbers use `companyKey` (case, punctuation, legal suffix ignored, HUB:43-52) over all college drives of the company plus drives through the lead's own recruiter account; `drives` counts PUBLISHED/CLOSED; `studentsHired` = distinct learners with an offer in the "counts as placed" statuses.
- Stage precedence (HUB:112-121): NOT_NOW(manual) > RECRUITED (placed student through the lead's own drives: their recruiter account or drives created since the lead was added) > DRIVE_PLANNED (any company drive in REQUEST/DRAFT/PUBLISHED) > JOINED (recruiterId) > INVITED (inviteSentAt) > CONTACTED (manual) > NEW.
- Invite status: JOINED > BOUNCED > CLICKED > SENT > NOT_SENT.
- Chips (HUB:125-137): OVERDUE (next follow-up before now); NO_NEXT_STEP (none open and stage not NOT_NOW/RECRUITED); QUIET (open stage, >= 14 days since last activity of any type else since createdAt; carries `quietDays`); BOUNCED; UNVERIFIED (joined and verification not VERIFIED_DOMAIN/TPO_APPROVED).

## 4. Intentional differences (vNext improvements to keep)
- DB-level race safety: partial unique indexes on college email/phone, row locks, one-transaction writes with a durable NOTE audit per created or changed lead.
- Optimistic concurrency on bulk stage/owner (`expectedStage`, `expectedOwnerMembershipId`) with per-lead results.
- Owner list limited to active placement staff with active users; invite tokens SHA-256 hashed with 7-day TTL, rotation on re-issue, invite activity attributed to the staff user.
- Phone normalised first then 8 to 10 digits (T-S2-02); source accepted by code or label.
- Import is all-or-nothing per transaction with in-file duplicate detection and per-row status; strict CSV parsing and size limits.
- Same-origin check on mutating routes; GET handlers read-only.
