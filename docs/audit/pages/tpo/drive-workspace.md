## 4. Intentional differences (vNext better or deliberate)
- Lifecycle actions use stale-guard previews: expected counts and `expectedUpdatedAt` reject a confirm if the drive changed (`tpo-drive-close-preview-form.tsx:20-33`, `tpo-drive-cancel-form.tsx:33-45`), mandatory 10+ character audited reasons, and REOPEN (not in legacy). Keep, but add the legacy cascade (TPO-drive-workspace-17, 18).
- Round results are staged per participant with candidate message required for fail, hold and absent, waitlist rank required for hold, and applied results immutable (`placement-drive-controls.tsx:715-730`); legacy stages by roll or sheet.
- Round schedule shows persisted venue collision warnings (`tpo-schedule-conflicts-service`) and a candidate schedule readiness view that refuses to invent slots; legacy has only the blocked-date check.
- Company share has an active-link limit, a status filter, history pages, `Copy URL` fallback and a separate access audit with CSV (`share-audit`), and a recruiter-visible preview of what will be shared; the legacy page only lists links.
- Offer register keeps durable versions with evidence pages and a joining tracker with history; CSV is formula-neutralised with a truncation row.
- Applicant filters live in the URL with validated values and an invalid-filter page that loads no private data (`applicants-pipeline/page.tsx:29-48`).
- Withdrawing an offer or accepting on a learner's behalf is refused in bulk withdraw (`placement-bulk-offer-withdraw.tsx:103`) where legacy allows `Company says accepted`; confirm with the owner before adding it back (TPO-drive-workspace-51).
