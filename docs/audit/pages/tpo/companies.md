## 4. Intentional differences (improvements over legacy to keep unless the owner objects)
- `/archived`, `/manage`, `/people`, `/contact-log`, `/feedback`, `Add company`, the directory and the hiring pulse are vNext additions. Legacy has no TPO archive, relationship-edit or contact log pages; there TPO adds contacts only through Engage and logs activity on the lead detail. Decision basis: owner's S4 in scope rule and the audited-writes pattern (confirmations with reason, optimistic `expectedUpdatedAt` tokens, same-origin checks, tenant scoping).
- Archive blocks while draft/published drives exist; global company name and domain are not editable by a college (shared across colleges); legacy has no such guard because it had no archive.
- Merge and duplicate handling: legacy TPO merges spellings silently by `companyKey` and shows "also typed as"; the Super Admin owns real merges (`/superadmin/companies`, other agent). vNext keeps the same silent key merge on the name page and the Engage tab and deliberately does not merge CRM names into canonical IDs on the directory ("CRM name only · not linked").
- Accepted-offer and confirmed-join counts (Offers, Accepted, Confirmed joins) are extra facts not in legacy.
- Time zone shown explicitly as IST.
