- SUPER_ADMIN only (impersonation additionally requires the platform admin id 0, `S/Faculty.js` `isSuperAdmin`); backend `hasRole(SUPER_ADMIN)`. Audit rows for every action (shown through the Audit trail modal and Activity Log). Impersonation swaps access/refresh tokens and shows an "Exit impersonation" control in the header (`pages/_app.js`).

## 4. Intentional differences (vNext additions or deliberate redesigns)
- Canonical person + college membership model: one identity can hold several roles and memberships (`CP/person-membership-actions.tsx`): "Manage roles" (membership-scoped; SUPER_ADMIN never assignable), suspend with reason, terminal "End membership". Legacy has one account per person with a single type.
- "Users & Roles" directory (`A/people/directory/page.tsx`) with Role / Membership status / Platform status filters, 30/page, CSV with formula neutralisation, contact masking outside the selected college, and "Users without college memberships" for global identities (`A/people/unassigned/page.tsx`). No legacy equivalent.
- Contact details (email/phone) are masked in lists and shown only inside an explicitly selected college; legacy shows everyone's mobile and email in rosters.
- No hard delete of people: ending a membership keeps history (legacy hard-deletes students and faculty after checks).
- Recruiter access is tied to a company-college relationship (a grant to a concrete relationship) instead of a free recruiter-college link; includes ARCHIVED/DISABLED states, page-size selector and a status filter that legacy lacks; "Terminate all sessions" wording describes the blast radius ("including sessions used for other memberships").
