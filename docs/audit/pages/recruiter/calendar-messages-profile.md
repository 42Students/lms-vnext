## 4. Intentional differences (vNext better than, or deliberately different from, legacy)

- Multi-college recruiter accounts: vNext resolves one of several active grants (chooser and `?collegeId=`), legacy had one college per session. The "choose a college" and "No active college access" states replace `NoCollegeSession`. Consistent with the identity model in docs/decisions; keep.
- Calendar: week/month/agenda views, category filter, jump-to-date, summary cards, "Results due" panel, "Next event", CSV export, IST display, mobile day list. Legacy had a week board only.
- Conversations: search, kind and unread filters, sort order, paging, bulk "mark visible unread as read", drive-proposal markers, optimistic reply feedback, GET never writes. Legacy had a client-side search only.
- Messages notifications inbox: grouped by drive, search, CSV export, per-row read (not a legacy feature; keep once Messages points to conversations, gap 08).
- Company & Team: team search, pagination and CSV export, hiring footprint with recent drives, company report filters and CSV export, page-size control.
- Profile: optimistic-versioned saves, read-only grant table with relationship status.
- `/hr-invite/[token]` kept as a redirect to `/recruiter-invite/[token]` (legacy rename), `/hr/*` kept beside `/recruiter/*`.
- Invite landing records the first open through a separate POST so GET stays read-only; recruiter not-hiring and unsubscribe are wired.
