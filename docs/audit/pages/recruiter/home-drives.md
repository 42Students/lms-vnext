## 4. Intentional differences (vNext improvements or approved deviations)

- Multi-college `?collegeId=` model: vNext lets one account hold several active grants and picks a college by query or chooser; legacy dev is one college per session (sign-in on the college address) and the chooser exists only in prod-2026. Consistent with the single-account design (`docs/parity/hr.md`, "Recruiter sessions are college-scoped"); not a gap.
- Drives list improvements over legacy: employment, work mode and deadline filters, sort options, 20-per-page pagination with totals, a deadline-attention panel, CSV export of matching drives, server-side search. Legacy has client-side title search and no pagination.
- Today extras: results-due list with urgency chips, "Replies to read" and "Waiting" thread signals without message bodies, college switch pills, link to Hiring activity.
- Result staging: per-candidate score, reason category, waitlist rank and learner-safe message with validation (non-pass needs a message, HOLD needs rank); legacy sends outcome only. Applied results are locked with an explanation.
- Offers: summary cards, state filter, CSV export, Letter-of-intent badge; read-only by design (placement team issues and withdraws).
- Rounds: schedule view with potential time-overlap detection; optimistic `updatedAt` conflict checks on edits.
- Draft form: reorder and duplicate rounds, compensation review warnings, copy-to-clipboard of the validated draft, idempotent proposal submission, a proposals history page, and a "Record an outside-platform drive" form (registered migration 0079). Legacy has none of these for recruiters.
- Applicant exports mask contact details by default and are formula-neutralised.
- N/A items named in the assignment: bulk actions, spreadsheet grid and company share links are TPO-side legacy features; recruiters have no equivalent in lms-dev-2026, and vNext adds none. The company share page (`/company-share`) is a public surface covered by its own audit group.
- Approved by owner decision 13 (exact wording): headings "Drives", "Calendar", "Messages", "Profile", "Request a drive" and the five-row sidebar are verified correct on main (`recruiter-page-copy.ts`, `shell-model.ts:205-211`). Remaining wording gaps above (statuses, tabs, empty states) are still open.
