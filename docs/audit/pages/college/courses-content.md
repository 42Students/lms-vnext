## 4. Intentional differences

- Course, Offering, Section Delivery and Teaching Assignment are separate persisted entities in vNext (cohort/period-specific offerings, exact-section deliveries, history-preserving `End assignment`), replacing the legacy course-to-class implicit model. Only the missing legacy capabilities above are gaps.
- Archive instead of delete across courses, offerings and assignments; assignment `Reassign` with audit trail and optimistic stamps is an improvement over delete-and-recreate.
- Metric cards on the Courses page and links from assignments to the offering are vNext additions.
