## 2. vNext behaviour

### 2.0 Routes and guards
- All routes require an active membership (`redirect("/dashboard")` otherwise, e.g. `V/app/(authenticated)/college/projects/page.tsx:35-38`); detail pages use `requireProjectManageAccess` (`admin-project-section-page.tsx:31-34`). Shared Super Admin components are reused with `basePath="/college/projects"`; the nav component hides itself on College routes (`project-admin-nav.tsx:23-26`).

### 2.1 Workspaces (`/college/projects`)
- Title `Workspaces` (no description), `CreateProjectControl` (dialog `Create Project` from a published template assigned to the college), links `Split class into teams / Add whole class` and `Bulk mentor reassignment`; filters `Search` (`Workspace title or slug`), status (`All statuses` + Planned/Active/Completed/Archived, shared list), page size; card grid (title, summary, status badge, `Mentor`, `Members`, `Progress`, `Updated`, `Open workspace`, archive/restore control `Archive`/`Restore`). Pagination previous/next with totals. No CSV.
- `Create Project` fields: template, title, summary and a few more (`project-create-control.tsx:164-290`). The class-team creator (`class-team-workspace-creator.tsx`) splits a class into balanced teams with roster refresh, role policy and preview token.

### 2.2 Workspace detail (`/college/projects/[projectId]/[section]`)
- Ten tabs `Summary` ... `Audit` (same names as legacy) inside `ProjectWorkspace` in `ADMIN` mode (`project-workspace.tsx:51-79,169`). Header: title, summary, status badge, `Archive`/`Restore`. Archived workspaces show a read-only banner. Metrics `Tasks`, `Sprints`, `Releases`, `Final submission`. Admin-only controls in the file: add/remove/change project members (`AddProjectMemberControl`, `ProjectMemberActions`; blocked for learners when the roster comes from a managed class team), `ProjectDeliveryReopenControl`, readiness override (`ProjectReadinessOverrideForm`). Sprints, tasks, resources, meetings and delivery are read views for the admin; their write controls are mentor/learner-mode only (`mode === "MENTOR"` at `:508,565,714`).

### 2.3 At Risk, Readiness, Submissions, Templates, Audit
- `At-risk projects`: description `Active workspaces with seven days of persisted inactivity or final delivery waiting for review.`; a warning that the legacy blocker text signal is not persisted; filters; one card per workspace (mentor, last persisted activity, `N signal(s)` + reason chips). Pagination.
- `Submission readiness`: per-workspace cards (progress, tasks, sprints, releases) with `Manage` to `/college/projects/[projectId]/readiness` (override form: state `Force unlock`/`Force block`/`Clear`, note required except for clear, 2000 chars).
- `Project submissions`: list of final-delivery versions with filters (DRAFT, SUBMITTED, CHANGES_REQUESTED, APPROVED), notice that Under Review/Locked/Reopen are not representable; read-only.
- `Template catalogue` (`Published project templates assigned to this college.`): cards with `View template`; detail page. `Project audit`: filters (action, actor, range) and table.

## 4. Intentional differences (keep)

- Projects are instantiated from the exact published template version assigned to the college (immutable assignment), with team workspaces generated from managed class teams and balanced splits, role policy, preview tokens and idempotent create.
- Archive with restore instead of soft-delete; archived workspaces are read-only with a clear banner.
- At Risk and Submissions report only persisted facts (the pages say what is not representable) instead of inferring legacy states.
- Optimistic concurrency stamps (`expectedUpdatedAt`) on archive and reopen; mentor/learner-mode controls keep the execution data owned by those roles.
