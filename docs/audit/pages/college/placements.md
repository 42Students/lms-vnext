## 4. Intentional differences (keep)

- Placement model is season- and relationship-based (employer relationship per college with trust and owner, seasons, evidence-based eligibility, offers with lifecycle and joining outcomes) instead of the legacy flat job/company tables. Status names `APPLIED..HIRED` are replaced by application (SUBMITTED/WITHDRAWN) plus screening and offer states; COL-placements-01 asks only that the College Admin can act on them.
- Every list is college-scoped and bounded (25 per page, capped CSV with truncation notice, formula-neutral cells), invalid filter queries are rejected with a clear message, and counts state exactly what they measure ("not a sequential funnel").
- Drive archive instead of delete, Close with consequence preview, and no cross-college company directory.
- The Analytics page links to the department evidence report; `Placement Evidence by Department` is a vNext addition (see `docs/parity/college-admin.md`).
