## 1. Legacy behaviour

### 1.1 Profile verification (`/college-admin/verification`, lms-dev-2026 only; `LFE/components/verification/VerificationWorkspace.jsx`, `LBE` `ProfileVerificationService`)
- `PageShell` title `Profile verification`, subtitle `Check each student's education and identity against their documents. Verified profiles are what companies and the placement cell rely on.` Cannot-verify state: Alert `You cannot verify profiles here` + server reason; error `Could not check your verification access` / `Could not load the queue` with `Retry`.
- Setting card (College Admin and Super Admin only): switch `The placement cell can verify profiles` / `Placement cell admins get this same page. Every decision is recorded with the name of who made it.`; toasts `The placement cell can now verify profiles` / `Only college admins verify profiles now`; failure `Could not save` with rollback; `Retry setting`.
- Views `Waiting (N)`, `Sent back (N)`, `Verified (N)`; quick filters `A marksheet missing`, `Updates to verified details`, `Waiting over a week` with counts and `Nothing unusual: no missing marksheets, updates or old ones.`; cap notice `Showing the first 1,000 students in this view`; data table with saved views, search (name, roll, branch), filter panel (`What to check`: Section, Kind (First check / Update to verified details), Marksheets, Waiting since; `Whose`: Batch, Branch), column chooser (`Student`, `Roll`, `Batch`, `Branch`, `Section`, `Status` New/Update/Sent back/Verified, `Marksheets` `N of M missing`/`All N uploaded`, `Sent|Verified|Sent back` age, `By`, `Reason sent back`), export `verification-{view}`, row button `Review`/`Open`. Empty `Nobody is waiting` / `Students appear here when they send their education or identity.`
- Act on selection/all (`Act on N selected` / `Act on all N`): `Review one by one` (opens the first, drawer shows `3 of 12`, `Next`/`Finish`, auto-advances after a decision, `Stopped at N of M. The other K are still in the queue.`), `Verify without opening` (acknowledgement `I have checked their documents another way ...`; checks with a missing document are left out by name; consequence text; updates replace verified values), `Send back` (one reason to all; per-section quick reasons).
- Student drawer: per section card `Education` / `Identity` with status tag (New, Update, Sent back, Verified, Not sent yet); `An update to verified details` banner with changed values highlighted; Education table `Level`, `Institution`, `Year`, `Marks` (grade / max, grading type), `Marksheet` `Open` (private link, `Not uploaded` in red), `Resume: Open resume`; Identity `Aadhaar` / `PAN` last four digits with `Open document` (`Numbers show only the last four digits; compare them with the document.`); `Verified by {name} on {date}`; buttons `Verify education|identity` and `Send back`; `History` timeline of every event (sent by the student, update sent, verified by, sent back by + note). Send-back modal `Send {Section} back`: `The student sees your reason and fixes it on their profile.`, section-specific quick reasons (marksheet unclear/cropped, marks do not match, marksheet missing; document does not match the number, wrong document, unclear), min 3 characters. Toasts `{Section} verified. The student has been told.`, `Sent back. The student sees your reason.`, `N verified. The students have been told.`.

## 4. Intentional differences (keep)

- Verification is queue-and-version based (stale-version protection, server-side paging and filters, receipts for private documents, explicit bulk preview with per-row outcomes) instead of a client-side 1,000-row table; document links never expose storage keys.
- The Activity Log reads immutable persisted events with severity, module and IST filters, a detail page per event, and a capped CSV export that omits IP addresses.
- The college contact profile, branding and domain pages give a College Admin self-service over college-facing details that legacy handled by support (subject to the decisions above).
- Profile shows account facts from the identity module and links to account security instead of embedding a CV-style profile.
