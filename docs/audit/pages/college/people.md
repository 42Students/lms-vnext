## 4. Intentional differences

- Membership-level lifecycle (`Suspend`/`Reactivate`/`End`) with reasons and activity events replaces user-level suspension and hard delete (identity can span colleges); student and faculty rows link to a detail page with attendance, enrollments and teaching history (vNext improvements).
- The student CSV import is dry-run first with a mandatory acknowledgment and per-row `IMPORTED`/`PARTIAL`/`FAILED`; this safety stays but must adopt legacy inputs (COL-people-05).
- Faculty `Reassign` moves assignments with schedule-conflict checks and optimistic stamps, and the Student groups link from People Directory is a vNext addition.
- Role grants through `Manage roles` are a vNext capability (a person may hold several roles in one college); see COL-people-20 for the permission decision.
